隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 26 日

本政策说明 (以下称"我们")运营的 服务如何收集、使用和保护你的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 账户信息：你登录时提供的邮箱；使用 Google 登录时，Google 向我们提供的邮箱(我们不获取你的 Google 密码或其他资料)。
  • 你上传的内容：地图图片及你填写的参数(经纬度范围、色带等)。
  • 生成的数据：气候、资源、演化结果、导出文件等。
  • 使用记录：任务记录、算力点用量、访问时间与 IP 地址，用于计费、限流与防止滥用。
  • Cookie：只使用一个必要的登录会话 Cookie，以及记住语言选择的本地存储。我们不使用广告或跨站追踪 Cookie。

2. 我们如何使用信息

仅用于：提供与改进服务、计算与计费用量、发送登录链接及与账户相关的必要通知、保障安全与防止滥用、遵守法律要求。我们不出售你的个人信息，也不使用你上传的地图训练机器学习模型。

3. 第三方服务

  • Cloudflare：网络加速与安全防护，会处理你的访问请求(包括 IP 地址)。
  • 邮件服务商：发送登录邮件。
  • Google：仅在你选择 Google 登录时。
  • AI 识图服务商：仅在你主动点击"AI 识图"时，你上传的图片会发送给我们配置的多模态模型服务商用于识别；不使用该功能则不会发送。
  • 支付服务商：开通付费套餐时，支付信息由支付服务商直接处理，我们不保存你的银行卡信息。

4. 保存与删除

你的世界、演化与导出文件会一直保存，直到你删除它们或删除账户。导出包可能在一段时间后清理(可随时重新导出)。删除账户后，我们会删除你的上传内容与生成数据；备份中的副本会在备份轮换周期(最长约 7 天)后清除。用量与审计记录可能按法律或财务要求保留更长时间。

5. 你的权利

你可以随时查看和删除自己的世界与演化，导出你的数据，或通过 要求访问、更正或删除你的个人信息，我们会在合理时间内处理。

6. 安全

全站使用 HTTPS；登录会话与接口密钥只以哈希形式存储；管理后台有独立的访问控制。但任何网络服务都无法保证绝对安全。

7. 未成年人

本服务不面向 13 周岁以下的儿童。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们删除。

8. 政策变更

政策如有重要变更，我们会在网站上提示，并更新上方的日期。

9. 联系我们

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how ("we") collects, uses and protects your information when you use .

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you sign in with; if you use Google sign-in, the email address Google shares with us (we never receive your Google password or other profile data).
  • Content you upload: map images and the parameters you enter (bounds, colour ramps, etc.).
  • Generated data: climate, resources, simulation results and export files.
  • Usage records: jobs, unit usage, access times and IP addresses, used for billing, rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Cookies: a single essential sign-in session cookie, plus local storage to remember your language. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

Only to provide and improve the service, meter and bill usage, send sign-in links and essential account notices, keep the service secure, and comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information and do not use your uploaded maps to train machine-learning models.

3. Third parties

  • Cloudflare: content delivery and security; processes your requests, including your IP address.
  • Email provider: to deliver sign-in emails.
  • Google: only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • AI map-reading provider: only when you click "AI map reading" is the uploaded image sent to the multimodal model provider we have configured; otherwise nothing is sent.
  • Payment provider: for paid plans, payment details are handled directly by the payment provider; we do not store your card details.

4. Retention and deletion

Your worlds, runs and exports are kept until you delete them or your account. Export packages may be cleaned up after a while (you can export again at any time). When you delete your account we delete your uploads and generated data; copies in backups are removed when backups rotate (at most about 7 days). Usage and audit records may be kept longer where required for legal or accounting reasons.

5. Your rights

You can view and delete your worlds and runs at any time, export your data, or contact to access, correct or delete your personal information. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Security

The whole site uses HTTPS; sign-in sessions and API keys are stored only as hashes; the admin console has separate access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If we make material changes we will announce them on the site and update the date above.
